Create a complete, beautiful, premium and highly interactive birthday website called “ANUVERSE 💜 — A LITTLE WORLD MADE JUST FOR YOU”.

This website is a special birthday gift from a brother to his younger sister, Anu. The goal is to combine a dreamy anniversary-style aesthetic, cinematic scrolling, heartfelt letters, funny sibling moments, interactive games, hidden surprises, birthday animations and a memorable emotional ending.

The website must feel personal, magical, playful and beautifully designed — not like a generic birthday template.

IMPORTANT: Build a continuous scrolling experience with multiple full-screen scenes. Do not use a dashboard as the main layout. The visitor should naturally scroll from one scene to the next, with each section feeling like a new chapter in a magical story.

## 1. VISUAL IDENTITY

Use a dreamy purple and lavender universe:
- Midnight-purple backgrounds, lavender gradients and glowing moons.
- Tiny twinkling stars, floating particles, butterflies and gentle sparkles.
- Glassmorphism panels, elegant typography and soft glowing text.
- Beautiful envelope, gift box, moon and cake illustrations.
- Cinematic transitions, subtle parallax and scroll-triggered reveals.
- A mixture of peaceful aesthetic scenes and playful interactive moments.

Keep the design polished, lightweight, mobile-first and responsive. Every section should have its own composition while maintaining the same visual identity.

## 2. OPENING SCENE — A LITTLE MAGIC ✨

Create a full-screen midnight-purple sky with a glowing moon, slowly moving stars and soft lavender clouds.

Display one line at a time:

“Some people make ordinary days feel special…”

Pause briefly, then reveal:

“Today, this little universe belongs to one special person.”

Finally show:

“HAPPY BIRTHDAY, ANU 💜”

Add a glowing “ENTER YOUR UNIVERSE” button that smoothly scrolls to the next scene.

Include subtle ambient animations and a visible scroll indicator.

## 3. THE STORY UNFOLDS — SCROLLING EXPERIENCE 🌙

Create a beautiful continuous vertical story with at least eight distinct scenes.

As the user scrolls:
- Background colours and lighting gradually change.
- Stars move at different depths to create a parallax effect.
- Text and illustrations appear with smooth reveal animations.
- Small interactive objects appear throughout the journey.
- A discreet progress indicator shows how far the visitor has travelled.

Avoid repeating the same card layout in every section. Use different visual compositions, spacing and animations to make each chapter feel unique.

## 4. THE SIBLING CHAOS ZONE 😂

Suddenly change the peaceful atmosphere into a playful purple comedy scene.

Display animated messages:
- “Birthday update installed successfully.”
- “Drama levels: still questionable.”
- “Annoying everyone: lifetime subscription activated.”
- “Official title: Queen of Chaos 👑”

Add a “Reveal Another Roast” button that displays a new random, affectionate birthday joke each time it is clicked.

Include funny pop-up-style messages and little animated stickers. Keep the humour loving and suitable for a brother and sister.

## 5. INTERACTIVE MINI-GAMES 🎮

Blend three fully playable mini-games naturally into the scrolling story.

GAME A — CATCH THE STARS
The player has 20 seconds to catch falling stars and gifts. Include a timer, score, increasing difficulty, keyboard and touch controls, and a replay button.

GAME B — THE SIBLING QUIZ
Create five funny, editable multiple-choice questions about sibling chaos. Show the score, playful reactions and a funny result title.

GAME C — UNLOCK THE MYSTERY GIFT
Display three beautiful gift boxes. Each box opens with an animation and reveals a different joke, birthday surprise or heartfelt note.

Allow visitors to skip any game and continue scrolling. Do not force them to complete every challenge to reach the birthday message.

## 6. HIDDEN LITTLE SURPRISES 🦋

Scatter tiny clickable stars, butterflies, sparkles and hidden icons throughout the website.

When clicked, they reveal small surprises such as:
- A funny sibling joke.
- A cute animated message.
- A secret birthday wish.
- A virtual award certificate.
- A special message from her brother.

Add a simple hidden surprise counter. Make these elements discoverable but never necessary for completing the website.

## 7. A LETTER JUST FOR ANU 💌

Transition into a calm lavender scene with a glowing envelope floating beneath the moon.

Display:

“Not every feeling fits inside a joke…”

Invite the visitor to tap the envelope. Animate it opening and reveal a beautifully formatted letter, appearing line by line.

Use this starter message:

Make the letter fully editable so her brother can personalise it. Do not invent real childhood memories or personal details.

## 8. THE MAKE-A-WISH SCENE 🎂

Create a gorgeous birthday cake with glowing candles, delicate lavender decorations and floating stars.

Allow the visitor to tap individual candles to light them. Add a “MAKE A WISH” button.

When pressed:
- Animate the candles and cake.
- Trigger a beautiful confetti celebration.
- Fill the scene with shooting stars, glowing particles and floating birthday wishes.
- Display “THE UNIVERSE IS CELEBRATING YOU, ANU! 💜”

Include a replayable cake interaction and an accessible skip option.

## 11. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

- Use React and CSS in the existing Replit environment where appropriate.
- Make every button, game, letter, gift box, candle and secret interaction functional.
- Use smooth scrolling and scroll-triggered animations without trapping the visitor in a section.
- Make all content and controls responsive on phones, tablets and desktops.
- Include working game timers, score tracking, win/lose states and restart functionality.
- Avoid browser alert boxes; use custom in-page pop-ups.
- Make the sister's name, quiz questions, jokes and brother's letter easy to edit.
- Use lightweight animations and avoid unnecessary external APIs, databases, logins or paid services.
- Preserve progress or simple settings in browser storage where useful.
- Include a clear way to skip games and reach the final message.
- Check for console errors, broken interactions, mobile overflow and scrolling issues.
- Do not invent Anu's age, photos, personal memories or preferences.
- Keep all messages strictly brother-sister themed, with funny teasing and genuine affection.

The final result should feel like a beautiful interactive birthday movie, a mini-game adventure and a heartfelt digital gift combined into one continuous scrolling website. Prioritise emotional storytelling, magical visuals, playful interactions and a memorable final scene.
